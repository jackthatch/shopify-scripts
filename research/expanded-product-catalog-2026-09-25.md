# Expanded home and seasonal product catalog

Research date: September 25, 2026. Market: United States / USD.

## Scope and recommendation

70 distinct product directions: 10 in each of the seven user-requested sections. This expands the earlier assortment target. Home means entryway and general decorative accessories; living area and entertainment means lounge styling, media organization, games, and hosting. Kitchen includes dining essentials. Outdoor living and gardens remain one combined section. Seasonal has three Halloween, three Thanksgiving/autumn, and four Christmas directions.

Use warm woods, cream, muted green, earthy brown, restrained black, and antique-gold finishes to make the catalog cohesive. Finish/color choices in this document are merchandising proposals, not proof that each linked supplier offers them. Garage is positioned around attractive household organization.

This is a research catalog, not 70 qualified suppliers or a launch-ready inventory. Retail references establish comparable assortment, not permission to resell the brand or copy its design/media. Every product has a source link, evidence type, and an unresolved sourcing check. No purchase, supplier outreach, account signup, Shopify import, or advertising was performed.

### Evidence and priorities

- **Direct supplier lead (11)**: specific product page found; stock, exact variant, media rights, delivered cost, and fulfillment still require verification. Some leads are explicitly deferred.
- **Supplier category lead (5)**: matching listings found in a sourcing platform's category; select and verify an exact product next.
- **Retail reference (54)**: comparable product or category offered by a retailer/manufacturer. An authorized supplier or suitable original alternative still needs sourcing.
- **First**: my priority for the next sourcing pass because of brand fit and generally simpler fulfillment. This does not mean validated demand or permission to publish.
- **Later**: additional qualification is material, such as fragile/large items, electrical details, installation, personalization, or holiday delivery.

Supplier prices are public displayed prices, not approved US landed quotes. Blank or default-zero shipping fields are not free-shipping confirmation. Retail page availability and sale prices can be inconsistent; no live-stock claims are made here. Suggested pairs/sets are proposed selling formats; the exact supplier quantity must be confirmed. All 70 landed costs are intentionally unknown in the JSON companion.

## Findings that affect selection

1. **Small coordinated goods are the clearest starting point.** Covers, table linens, trays, frames, caddies, and coasters can express the brand across rooms. Their demand and delivered economics still need checking.
2. **The garage section has a weaker direct connection to a lavish-home positioning.** Keep it visually consistent and about organized spaces. Avoid turning it into a general tools store. The branded systems reviewed are references; their accessories may require proprietary rails or panels.
3. **The source quality varies.** The [Doba 12-piece garage-hook lead](https://www.doba.com/product/uyqdClIrjcba/dropshipping-12-piece-garage-hook-heavy-duty-utility-steel-garage-storage-hooks-wall-mounted-garage-hooks-and-organizers-for-organizing-power-tools-ladders-bulk-items-bicycles-ropes-and-more-equipment.html) says discontinued, so it is not included as an active supplier. Category alternatives are explicitly unqualified.
4. **Product names can mislead.** The cushion lead's packing list says cover even though a field mentions filler. The runner title mentions linen while specifications say cotton. The remote organizer says leather without sufficient composition evidence. Resolve these before writing listings.
5. **Supplier item prices do not establish margins.** The inexpensive spiderweb coaster and cushion-cover leads may still have poor economics after individual shipping, fees, and advertising. Treat them as possible add-ons or coordinated sets, not automatic paid-ad products.
6. **Seventy listings do not require seventy paid tests.** The catalog target is now 70, but the existing $100 budget and no-samples policy remain unchanged. The earlier 3–5-product shortlist can serve as the first activation wave, with one marketing priority. Building the broader catalog is sequential work, not approval to spend more.

## Holiday handling

The ten seasonal products are ten total, not ten per holiday. Halloween is October 31, Thanksgiving November 26, and Christmas December 25 in 2026. Holiday listings need delivery before the customer's decorating date, not merely the celebration date. Derive each cutoff from verified processing plus transit plus an explicit delay buffer. A domestic warehouse claim alone does not establish a delivery promise.

Cross-tag products where useful (for example K01 runner in Thanksgiving styling) without counting one SKU twice in the 70-product plan. Neutral and botanical runners here are separate proposed designs, not duplicate colors counted as different product types.
