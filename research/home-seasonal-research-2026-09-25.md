## Recommendation

Build around warm, coordinated living spaces, dining tables, and welcoming entryways. Use cream, brown, rust, muted green, antique gold, and black. Seasonal collections can change accents while retaining the same home aesthetic.

Prioritize a textured table runner, cushion covers, and a small seasonal accent. Research outdoor lanterns and Christmas greenery alongside them. These are sourcing candidates, not validated winners. Public retail assortment establishes that comparable products are marketed, not sales volume or willingness to buy from this store. No live demand-volume study was completed in this pass.

## Proposed initial assortment

This is a five-product research shortlist, not an instruction to publish all five:

1. Neutral woven table runner: priority creative/listing draft, because it spans everyday home styling and holiday entertaining.
2. Coordinated textured cushion-cover pair: everyday base with seasonal color variants.
3. Black or muted velvet pumpkin set: Halloween/Thanksgiving accent, conditional on delivery timing.
4. Knitted stocking: Christmas candidate, conditional on personalization and fulfillment checks.
5. Warm outdoor lantern: outdoor candidate, conditional on the exact variant passing specification and cost review.

Christmas greenery is the backup seasonal direction if stocking personalization is too complex. The $52.81 garland lead is not selected for launch.

Do not impose the scripts' $49.99 minimum on every accessory. The $28 runner and $29.99 lantern comparisons argue for checking realistic market prices first. Coordinated pairs or bundles are hypotheses to test, not a justification for arbitrary markups; fulfillment must support combined shipping and correct quantities.

## Seasonal timing and delivery gates

As of September 25, Halloween is October 31, Thanksgiving is November 26, and Christmas is December 25, 2026. That leaves 36, 62, and 91 calendar days respectively. Customers typically need decorations before the celebration; the holiday date is not a suitable delivery target.

- Halloween: make this a small, conditional section. Prefer verified domestic inventory. Do not accept a Halloween-specific order until arrival before the buyer's needed decorating date is supportable.
- Thanksgiving: prioritize runners, cushion covers, and non-spooky pumpkins that can remain useful after October.
- Christmas: research now, with special attention to production lead time for personalized items. Separate covered-porch products from products documented for exposed outdoor use.
- Set each order cutoff from the desired arrival date minus verified processing, transit, and an explicit delay buffer. No universal shipping cutoff can be established from the public supplier pages reviewed.
